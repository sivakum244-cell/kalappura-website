<p>A houseboat cruise on the Alleppey (Alappuzha) backwaters is, for many people, the single most memorable part of a Kerala trip. But if it's your first time, it can be confusing: What do the boats actually include? How much should you pay? Is a day cruise enough, or do you need to stay overnight? This guide answers the questions we get asked most often — honestly, from the perspective of a family-run operator based in Alleppey.</p>

<h2>What is an Alleppey houseboat?</h2>
<p>Alleppey houseboats are converted <em>kettuvallam</em> — traditional Kerala cargo boats once used to move rice and spices through the backwaters. Today they're private, slow-moving boats with bedrooms, bathrooms, a living deck and a small kitchen, crewed by a captain, a cook and a helper. You don't share the boat with strangers; each houseboat is booked privately for your group.</p>

<h2>Where you'll actually cruise</h2>
<p>Most cruises start from the Alleppey (Alappuzha) area and move through <strong>Vembanad Lake</strong>, the canals of <strong>Kuttanad</strong> (the "rice bowl of Kerala"), and quiet village waterways lined with coconut palms and paddy fields. The pace is deliberately slow — the point is to watch village life go by, not to cover distance.</p>

<h2>Houseboat types and how many people they suit</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>1 bedroom houseboat</strong> — ideal for couples and honeymooners who want privacy.</li>
  <li><strong>2 bedroom houseboat</strong> — good for small families or two couples travelling together.</li>
  <li><strong>3 bedroom houseboat</strong> — suited to larger families and groups.</li>
</ul>
<p>You can see the specific boats, layouts and photos on our <a href="/alleppey-houseboats">Alleppey houseboats page</a>.</p>

<h2>Day cruise vs overnight stay</h2>
<p>This is the most common decision travellers face.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Day cruise</strong> — typically late morning to late afternoon. Great if you're short on time or nervous about staying on the water overnight. You still get lunch and the core backwater scenery.</li>
  <li><strong>Overnight stay</strong> — you board around midday, cruise through the afternoon, moor for the evening, have dinner on board, sleep in an air-conditioned bedroom, and wake to a still, misty morning on the lake. This is where the real magic happens.</li>
</ul>
<p>For most first-timers, we recommend at least one <a href="/overnight-houseboat">overnight houseboat stay</a>.</p>

<h2>What's included in the price?</h2>
<p>A genuine private houseboat rate should already cover an air-conditioned bedroom with a private bathroom, all meals cooked fresh on board (breakfast, lunch, evening tea with snacks, and dinner), the cruise itself, and the crew. Always confirm these are included before you pay so you're comparing like with like. For a full breakdown, see our <a href="/houseboat-prices">Alleppey houseboat price guide</a>.</p>

<h2>The food</h2>
<p>Food is a highlight, not an afterthought. Meals are typically traditional Kerala cuisine — rice, fish curry (fresh from the backwaters), thoran, avial, and seasonal vegetables, cooked to your preferred spice level. Vegetarian, vegan and other dietary needs can usually be accommodated if you mention them when booking.</p>

<h2>Best time to visit</h2>
<p>The most comfortable months are roughly <strong>October to February</strong>, with pleasant weather and clear skies. The monsoon months bring lush green scenery and lower prices, but heavier rain. Peak season, holidays and long weekends are busier and priced higher, so book ahead if you're travelling then.</p>

<h2>How to book without paying agent commission</h2>
<p>Many houseboat listings online are travel agents who add a commission on top of the boat owner's rate. Booking directly with the operator usually means a clearer price and better communication. You can check live availability and reserve on our <a href="/booking">booking page</a>, or message us on WhatsApp for a quick quote.</p>

<h2>A few practical tips</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Carry light cotton clothing, sunscreen and a hat — the deck gets sunny.</li>
  <li>Tell your operator about dietary needs in advance.</li>
  <li>Boats moor for the night (they don't cruise after dark) — this is a safety rule, not a limitation.</li>
  <li>Mobile signal can be patchy in some canals; treat it as part of the escape.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Is it safe?</strong> Yes — boats are crewed and moor overnight in calm waters.</li>
  <li><strong>Can we choose vegetarian or vegan food?</strong> Yes, with advance notice.</li>
  <li><strong>How far in advance should I book?</strong> For peak season and weekends, as early as possible; off-peak is more flexible.</li>
  <li><strong>What's the cheapest option?</strong> A 1 bedroom private boat is the most affordable, ideal for couples.</li>
</ul>
