# Anchor-Text Strategy & 6-Month Acquisition Plan

## Anchor distribution (target percentages)
| Anchor type | Examples | Target % |
|---|---|---|
| Brand | Kalappura Houseboats; Kalappura Houseboats & Tours | 40% |
| Naked URL / generic | alleppeyhouseboat.online; website; book here; learn more | 25% |
| Partial-match | Alleppey houseboat; Alappuzha houseboat; Kerala backwater houseboat | 20% |
| Long-tail / natural | houseboat experience in Alleppey; overnight houseboat in Alleppey; private houseboat cruise in Alappuzha | 12% |
| Exact-match (sparingly) | Alleppey houseboat booking | <=3% |

Rules:
- Brand + natural should dominate.
- Never repeat the same exact-match anchor across many sites.
- For editorial links, let the writer choose wording (most natural + safest).
- Directory/profile links = ALWAYS the brand name, never a keyword.

- New referring domains (from GSC / Ahrefs)
- % dofollow vs nofollow (nofollow is fine and natural)
- Referral traffic (GA4 -> Acquisition -> Referrals)
- Google Business Profile views/calls/direction requests
- Keyword movement for: Alleppey houseboat, Alappuzha houseboat, Alleppey houseboat price
